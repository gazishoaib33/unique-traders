TOTAL INVENTORY VALUE (add this formula in a cell below):
=SUMIF(N2:N100,"✓ OK",L2:L100)+SUMIF(N2:N100,"⚠ Low Stock",L2:L100)

Or simpler — just sum the whole column:
=SUM(L2:L100)

==============================================================
TAB 4: DAILY SUMMARY (Dashboard)
==============================================================

Add these in Google Sheets cells manually:

Cell A1: "Date"                   Cell B1: =TODAY()
Cell A2: "Total Products"         Cell B2: =COUNTA(Products!A2:A100)
Cell A3: "Total Stock Value"      Cell B3: =SUM(Products!L2:L100)
Cell A4: "Low Stock Items"        Cell B4: =COUNTIF(Products!N2:N100,"⚠ Low Stock")
Cell A5: "Today's Total Sales"    Cell B5: =SUMIF('Stock Out'!A2:A100,TODAY(),'Stock Out'!H2:H100)
Cell A6: "Total Due (all time)"   Cell B6: =SUM('Stock Out'!K2:K100)
Cell A7: "This Month Sales"       Cell B7: =SUMPRODUCT((MONTH('Stock Out'!A2:A100)=MONTH(TODAY()))*('Stock Out'!H2:H100))


==============================================================
CONDITIONAL FORMATTING RULES (Apply in Google Sheets):
==============================================================

1. Select column N (Status) in TAB 1
   → Format > Conditional formatting
   → Text contains "⚠ Low Stock" → Red background, white text
   → Text contains "✓ OK"        → Green background, dark text

2. Select column J (Current Stock) in TAB 1
   → Format > Conditional formatting
   → Less than or equal to column M → Red bold text


==============================================================
HOW TO UPDATE STOCK DAILY (Simple routine):
==============================================================

WHEN YOU SELL (Stock goes out):
  1. Open TAB 3 (Stock Out) → Add a new row with today's date, customer, product, qty, price
  2. Go to TAB 1 → Find that product → Increase "Stock Out" (column I) by the qty sold
  3. Current Stock (column J) auto-updates via formula ✓

WHEN STOCK ARRIVES (New delivery):
  1. Open TAB 2 (Stock In) → Add a row with date, product, qty received, cost
  2. Go to TAB 1 → Find that product → Increase "Stock In" (column H) by the qty received
  3. Current Stock (column J) auto-updates via formula ✓

CHECK DAILY:
  → Look at TAB 4 (Daily Summary) every morning
  → Any "⚠ Low Stock" alerts in TAB 1? Reorder that product!
